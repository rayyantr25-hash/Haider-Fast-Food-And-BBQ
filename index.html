About us

Fire, spice, and Malir.

Haider Fast Food & BBQ started with one grill and one simple idea: serve hot, generous food at a fair price, all night long. Today we feed families, friends and late-night crowds across Shah Faisal Town and Bagh-e-Ibrahim Society.

Our chicken tikka, seekh kabab and malai boti are marinated fresh every day and cooked over charcoal. For something quicker, we serve crispy broast, zinger burgers and a hearty baked lasagna.

We keep prices under Rs 1,000 a head, and we keep the kitchen open until 3 am.
